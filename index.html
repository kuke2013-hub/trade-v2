06 / TRANSPARENCY

What the algorithms actually do

Four fixed-rule baselines plus one data-trained historical model. The ML model never trades unless the holdout paper gate is passed.

Important limitations

This is a browser-run experiment. Trades are simulated from available Binance spot data, not an exchange paper account. Candlestick extrema do not reveal intraminute execution order, and simulated spread/slippage may differ materially from real fills. Missing data pauses decisions; the application never invents candles or results. The +2% target and −1.5% stop are experimental parameters; historic model labels use next-hour open and conservative 1h candles, not exact executions. No live execution, leverage, futures or shorts are supported.

Live market rankings refresh approximately every 60 seconds, and one-, three- and five-minute charts refresh every 60 seconds for visible pairs while this tab is open; background throttling and API limits may delay updates. The price/ranking scan runs every minute, but confirmed strategy signals only change when a new hourly candle closes. Automatic scanning is attempted periodically while this tab is open; background-tab throttling, disconnected internet, CORS limits, geographic restrictions and browser shutdown can interrupt monitoring. A persistent 24/7 service and independent out-of-sample backtests are required before considering a strategy validated.
